Every generation believes that their Saturday Night Live cast was the *best* and that the current cast is the *worst*. 

It just so happens that my generation's SNL cast (mid '90s) featured Chris Farley, Dana Carvey, Norm Macdonald, Phil Hartman, Adam Sandler, Mike Myers, Will Ferrell, and I have to stop now there's too many absolute murderers.[^1]

So, when I say that my generation's cast was the best, I'm actually correct. 😉 

I still catch the show, mostly through YouTube clips, and I have to agree with previous generations that it hasn't been truly funny for a very long time. Weekend Update still has its moments, but the cold opens have become laughless political gags[^2] and when was the last time an iconic character or sketch was created?[^3] The only modern classic I can think of is [Washington's Dream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JYqfVE-fykk), which wouldn't be what it is without Nate Bargatze's delivery.

All that being said... I loved this sketch with Marcello Hernández and (especially) Mikey Day[^4] so much *I'm blogging about it*. It's an absolute banger[^5] that hits on multiple levels.
